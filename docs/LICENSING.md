## Can I use it?

Yes. Run it, read it, change it, for personal or work use, free. The AGPL puts conditions on distributing it, not on using it.

## Can I fork it or self host it?

Yes. If you share your fork, or let other people use a modified version over a network, you must offer them the full source of your version under the AGPL too. Keep the [LICENSE](../LICENSE) and [NOTICE](../NOTICE) files.

## Can I put it inside a closed source product?

Not under the AGPL. Your product would have to be released under the AGPL as well. If that does not work for you, ask for a commercial license.

## Why is there a CLA?

The [Contributor License Agreement](../CLA.md) lets the copyright holder offer SWE Radar under terms other than the AGPL, such as a commercial license, or change the license later, without tracking down every past contributor. You keep the copyright on your contributions and are credited for them.

## How do I get a commercial license?

Open an issue, or reach the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/abhiramshaji-cyber).
